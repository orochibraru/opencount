# Your account

**Account** holds three sections: profile, security and appearance. Signing out
is at the bottom of it.

## Profile

Your name, as other members see it when they link you to a group, up to 80
characters. Your email is the one you sign in with; you can't change it
yourself.

## Security

- **Authenticator app**: confirm your password, scan the QR code with any app
  that makes 6-digit codes (or type the key), and enter a code to finish. Keep
  the backup codes it shows: each one signs you in once if you lose the device.
  At sign-in you can trust the device for 30 days.
- **Passkeys**: Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or a security key. Add as many
  as you like and remove the ones you no longer use. With passkey sign-in on, a
  passkey replaces your email and password at sign-in.

An admin can require either of them. Until you add what is required, every page
sends you here, with a note saying what is missing. The same happens when an
admin turns password sign-in off and you have no other way in: add a passkey,
and you are through. See [Sign-in and single sign-on](sign-in-and-sso).

## Appearance

Stored on your account, so it follows you to every device, and applied before
the page paints, so a reload never flashes the wrong theme.

| Setting   | Choices                                                                     |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accent    | Bordeaux (default), Green, Blue, Violet, Rose, Orange, Amber, Cyan, Neutral |
| Theme     | System (default), Light, Dark                                               |
| Dark mode | Dim (default), AMOLED black                                                 |
| Font      | Geist and Sora (default), System, Serif, Mono                               |
| Corners   | Rounded (default), Square                                                   |

Every colour in the app derives from the accent, so the whole interface shifts
with it. AMOLED black turns dark backgrounds pure black, which saves battery on
OLED phones.

## On a phone

Add OpenCount to the home screen (**Share, Add to Home Screen** in Safari,
**Install app** in Chrome) and it opens standalone, like a native app. The
bottom tab bar holds Groups and Account, plus Admin for admins. On a desktop, a
sidebar lists Groups, Admin and your groups, with your account in the menu under
your name at the bottom.

## Not there yet

Changing your password or your email, resetting a forgotten password, and
deleting your own account are not in the app yet. An admin can delete an account
from [the admin area](admin).
